The roof

Flat concrete slabs with deep overhangs shade the glass in summer and let the low winter sun in. 250 mm of insulation and hidden drainage keep the silhouette clean.

The walls

Monolithic reinforced concrete holds warmth in winter and keeps the rooms cool in summer. Ceilings rise to 3 metres throughout the house.

Glass and interior

Floor-to-ceiling triple glazing in oak frames, shaded by thermowood fins. Every room opens onto the garden or the courtyard.

The foundation

An insulated concrete slab, engineered for every European climate — from Nordic frost to Mediterranean heat.

Seven months from
contract to keys

We build across Europe. All you need is a plot of at least 900 m² (25 × 36 m).

  1. Month 1

    Contract and site survey

    We fix the price and the date, then survey your plot and stake out the house.

  2. Month 2

    Foundation

    An insulated concrete slab is poured and left to cure.

  3. Months 3–4

    Walls, roof and glazing

    Concrete walls and the flat roof go up, and triple glazing closes the house.

  4. Months 5–6

    Engineering and finishing

    Heating, plumbing and electrics, then floors, bathrooms and the kitchen.

  5. Month 7

    Interior and handover

    We finish the interior you chose and hand over the keys.
